About Me

With over 3 years experience as a professional gardener and RHS qualifications to boot, I can provide tailored advice and planting suggestions for your garden.

Green George provides a range of garden services including, but not limited to:

  • Lawn Care
  • Pruning
  • Weeding
  • Hedge Cutting
  • Planting
  • Mulching

I always encourage composting on site, as this is best for your garden. But, if needed I can remove green waste for an extra charge. I bring all my own hand tools but am always happy to use your own tools.
